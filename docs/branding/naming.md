# Naming

## Objective

The product name should become synonymous with action figure collecting.

The ambition is not to describe the application.

The ambition is to create a memorable brand capable of growing into the reference platform for collectors.

The name should feel equally natural on:

- a website
- a mobile application
- social media
- merchandising
- documentation
- conversations between collectors

The ideal outcome is for collectors to naturally say:

> "I already have it on ______."

---

# Naming Criteria

The final name should:

- Be memorable.
- Be easy to pronounce internationally.
- Be easy to spell.
- Feel premium.
- Age well.
- Scale beyond Star Wars.
- Scale beyond action figures.
- Feel like a platform rather than an application.
- Be emotionally connected to collecting.

Avoid names that are:

- overly descriptive
- franchise-specific
- difficult to remember
- technology-oriented
- temporary trends

---

# Naming Philosophy

Rather than describing features, the name should represent a collector's experience.

The strongest names usually evoke an emotion or a ritual instead of a functionality.

Examples outside the project:

- Discogs
- Letterboxd
- BrickLink
- Notion
- Reddit

None of them describe exactly what the product does.

Instead, they become brands.

---

# Inspiration

The project should feel inspired by the rituals of collecting.

Not by technology.

Not by databases.

Not by software.

The inspiration comes from the collector.

Examples of collector rituals:

- Looking at the cardback.
- Checking missing figures.
- Completing a collection.
- Organizing shelves.
- Discovering variants.
- Hunting for rare pieces.
- Comparing releases.
- Tracking progress.

## Cardback Check

Origin

Inspired by a collector ritual.

Many collectors used to turn the blister around, look at the cardback and mark the figures they already owned.

The expression "Cardback Check" captures that exact moment.

Pros

- Emotional.
- Collector-focused.
- Memorable.
- Immediately tells a story.

Cons

- Longer than "Cardback".
- Might work better as a feature name than the product itself.
